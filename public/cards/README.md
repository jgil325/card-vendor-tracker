# Demo artwork sources

Artwork is used to identify the fictional demo collection; it is not original artwork by this project. Pokémon card artwork belongs to its respective owners and is served by [TCGdex](https://tcgdex.dev/). One Piece card artwork belongs to its respective owners and the demo images are sourced from [TCGplayer](https://www.tcgplayer.com/).

All quantities, prices, vendors, and transactions in the demo are illustrative. No live market data is fetched. Private records and newly added cards use a neutral placeholder rather than assigning artwork that may not match their actual printing.
